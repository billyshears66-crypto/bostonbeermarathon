What It Is

A choose-your-own-distance route through Boston. Run the full course, half, or just meet along the way.

What It Is Not

Not a race. No registration, no timing, no official organization, and absolutely no promise that tomorrow will be productive.

How It Works

Pick a course. Run between stops. Have a beer or do not. Eat when needed. Hydrate when you remember.

Courses

Two ways through the city, both built for questionable pacing and better stories than splits.
