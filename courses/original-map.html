This is the mapped version of the Original Course. The full route is useful for seeing the big picture, but the leg-by-leg links are the better way to actually run it without wandering past a perfectly good bar.

Load the next leg before you leave each stop. Future-you may be brave, but future-you is not guaranteed to be better at navigation.
