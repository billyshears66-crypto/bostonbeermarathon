Home Waterfront Course How To Run It

This is the mapped version of the Waterfront Course. The full route shows the big picture, but the leg-by-leg links are the practical way to move from one stop to the next without accidentally turning the day into a sightseeing experiment.

Load the next leg before you leave each stop. The water views are nice, but they are not a navigation strategy.
