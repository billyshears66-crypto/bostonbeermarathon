The Waterfront Course

Harbor views, brewery stops, same questionable pacing.
Home Original Course

A blue-side spin through Boston's waterfront bars and breweries. This route trades the original crawl across town for harbor air, stadium energy, and a finish at Trillium.

Start at Sam Adams, keep the water close, and pretend the sea breeze counts as recovery.
